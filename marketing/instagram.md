Body:

Here is what nobody tells you about cloning your own voice: the expensive part is not the model, it is the habit of sending everything to it.

I split one audiobook by language. English, about 35 minutes, ran on a free open model on my own laptop. Korean, about 33 minutes, plus the two moments where Scripture is quoted plainly, went to a cloud clone. The result is one continuous file, crossfaded, normalised, and measured for six kinds of defects.

Zero defects. 30 minutes and 13 seconds. Four cents.

And the surprise: the free clone was closer to my actual timbre than the paid one. It just needed to be slowed to my own speaking pace, which the tool now does by default.

Code and docs are on GitHub. Link in bio.
