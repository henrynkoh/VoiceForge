# Threads

**Post 1**

I rendered a 30-minute audiobook in my cloned voice for $0.04.

The trick was not a cheaper model. It was refusing to send the whole book to the cloud.

**Post 2**

Same book, two builds:
- everything on ElevenLabs: 17,200 credits, $2.85
- English on a free local clone, Korean on the cloud: 229 credits, $0.04

English was 35 of the 68 minutes. That is where the money went.

**Post 3**

Two things I did not expect, both from measurement:
- the free local clone matched my timbre closer than the cloud one (0.959 vs 0.815 cosine)
- it read 35% too fast until I time-stretched it to my own 13.3 characters per second

Tool is open source: github.com/henrynkoh/VoiceForge
