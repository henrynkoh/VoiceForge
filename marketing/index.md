# VoiceForge marketing kit

Launch copy for nine channels, written to be pasted as-is. Every number in here is measured, not estimated, so it survives a sceptical reply.

The hook that does the work: **a 30-minute audiobook in your own voice for four cents, and the same book for free if you skip the cloud.**

| file | channel | language | format |
|---|---|---|---|
| [facebook.md](facebook.md) | Facebook page + groups | English | long post, 200-280 words |
| [instagram.md](instagram.md) | Instagram feed + Reels | English | caption, 5 slides, hook in line one |
| [threads.md](threads.md) | Threads | English | 3-post thread |
| [blogger.md](blogger.md) | Blogger | English | article, 700 words, SEO heading structure |
| [naver-blog.md](naver-blog.md) | Naver Blog | Korean | 1,200 words, sectioned for Naver's reader |

| [email.md](email.md) | Email campaign | English | 3-email sequence, D0 / D3 / D7 |

## Claims you can make safely

- 30 minutes of two-language narration came to 229 credits, about four cents, versus 17,200 credits for the all-cloud build.
- The English-only edition is 30:13 of audio, 22,960 characters, zero defects on the project's six-point gauge.
- Local narration is free, runs on CPU, and needs no account.
- A local clone matched the speaker's timbre more closely than the cloud clone (0.959 vs 0.815 cosine against his own recordings).

## Claims to avoid

- Do not promise the local voice is universally better; it is closer in timbre and flatter in delivery until the pace fix is on.
- Do not promise unlimited free Korean locally; that needs a multilingual model and more memory than a 3.8 GB container.
- Do not promise it works from a phone or without a computer.
