# Episode stings, English edition

Eight short pieces, recorded once each, used to open and close the four episodes.
Written for an English-only audience: no Korean, no bilingual framing.

## p1-open

Welcome to Rightly Dividing, a four-part study in the King James Bible. I am glad you are here. In this first part we ask one question of the whole Book: who is this written to? By the end you will see why so much of the confusion simply falls away once you check the address. So find a chair, bring your Bible, and let us open it together.

## p1-close

That is part one. In part two we come to the verse that gives this whole study its name, and to the workman who cuts a straight line through timber. Stay with me.

## p2-open

This is part two of Rightly Dividing. Now we come to the verse itself, Paul's charge to a young man named Timothy: Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

## p2-close

That is part two. In part three we look at two peoples in one Book, and why keeping them apart changes how you read everything else.

## p3-open

This is part three of Rightly Dividing. Two peoples, two callings, and one Lord. Then we take the questions people actually ask, the ones that keep honest readers awake at night.

## p3-close

That is part three. In the final part we look at two destinies, then at how you read your Bible tomorrow morning.

## p4-open

This is the last part of Rightly Dividing. Where is all of this going, and what do you do with it tomorrow morning? Let us finish the line we started.

## p4-close

That is the series. If this helped you, subscribe for more, and leave a comment with the passage you are studying this week. Until next time, open the Book, and divide it rightly.
