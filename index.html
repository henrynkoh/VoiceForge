Clone your voice. Narrate the book. Pay almost nothing.

A local-first narration studio that renders narration in your own cloned voice, measures every render for six defects, and packages the result as a book, a series, and upload-ready video. English runs free on your machine; only the passages that must be perfect go to the cloud.
